# FlowMoney private alpha

FlowMoney helps you understand recorded cash, review past transactions and plan around future commitments.
It does not hold your money, move money or ask for your bank login.

## Try these tasks

Start with the welcome walkthrough, or replay it from Settings → Help & feedback.
Tell us whether each section’s purpose is clear. You can skip the tour at any time.

1. Add your current account balances in Current Funds, then add expected income and an unpaid commitment.
   Keep balances up to date yourself; expected income is not available cash until it arrives.
2. Upload a digital PDF, CSV or XLSX statement. Image-only/scanned PDFs are unsupported. Confirm the
   statement currency and review the extracted rows. Exclude any rows you do not want; only selected,
   approved transactions are saved to history. A transfer received does not automatically mean salary.
3. Create a linked monthly plan using your existing records, or a standalone plan with its own starting
   amount. Standalone plans do not use your accounts or statement history. Planning does not move money.
4. Read Overview, then try a scenario. Explain what you think the safe-to-spend figure includes.
   Overview excludes category allocations in Planning; plans do not fund one another automatically.
5. Open Settings. Try appearance preferences and locate Help & feedback. Currency preference changes
   do not convert existing amounts with an exchange rate; review that confirmation carefully.

Email [ogabasyle@gmail.com](mailto:ogabasyle@gmail.com?subject=FlowMoney%20alpha%20feedback).
Describe the page, what you tried, what you expected and what happened. Include your browser/device,
but leave out passwords, bank credentials, account numbers and raw statements.

- Which label or figure was hardest to understand?
- How many statement rows needed correction, and what kind of correction?
- Could you finish a useful plan? Where did you hesitate?
- Did any screen overflow or become hard to use on your device?
- What is the one change that would make you return next week?

Settings lets you delete your FlowMoney records. That action does not delete your Clerk sign-in identity.
This is an alpha: use it to support your own planning and verify the information before relying on a figure.
